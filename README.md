## The fees that land after checkout

Every budget host has a second price list, and it rarely appears in the comparison table. Across the low-cost VPS market the recurring add-ons are:

- Control panel licences (cPanel, Plesk, DirectAdmin are usually separate)
- Automated backups and snapshots
- Extra IPv4 addresses
- Managed support on an unmanaged plan
- Migration of an existing site or server
- Security extras such as malware scanning or advanced DDoS filtering
- Bandwidth overage or fair-use throttling
- Advanced monitoring

Storage and traffic are where JustHost is straightforward. The port is unmetered, so there is no overage bill waiting for you, and NVMe is included in every plan rather than sold as an upgrade. Budget for a backup separately: if the host does not include snapshots on your plan, keep your own copy off the server. Anyone who has run a $2 VPS for a year will tell you the failure mode is not a slow disk, it is a dead machine with no copy of the data.

## Who should not buy a $2 VPS

An unmanaged VPS is a server you administer. If any of the following describes you, the cheap tier will cost you more in time than it saves in dollars:

- You do not want to touch a command line, a firewall, or an SSH key.
- You need Windows Server; budget Linux plans do not include a Windows licence.
- You need a fully managed host that patches and monitors for you.
- You are running a store where an hour of downtime loses orders.
- Your site is small and low-traffic enough that shared hosting is genuinely cheaper and simpler.

For those cases, the honest answer is to raise the budget. Managed VPS plans start where the cheap tier ends.

## FAQ

**Is the $1.99 JustHost plan enough for WordPress?**
No. 512 MB of RAM and 5 GB of NVMe leaves no room for PHP, MySQL, and a control panel together. For a small WordPress install, start at 2 GB, which means Arcturus at $5.53 or Arcturus Special at $3.21 on a 12-month term.

**Monthly or annual billing?**
Annual saves 20% and locks you in for a year. On Sirius that is $3.88 against $4.85, about $11.64 saved over twelve months. Pay monthly for the first month, confirm the node performs, then switch to annual.

**Will the price go up after the first term?**
JustHost's page does not list a renewal rate, so confirm it at checkout. Across the market this is the single most common surprise; several hosts double or triple the price after the intro period.

**Do I need to buy a control panel?**
Not for a VPN, a bot, or a single Docker app. A panel is worth the licence when you are running multiple sites and want email, DNS, and databases in one interface.

**Is a 10 Mbps port enough?**
It works out to roughly 4 TB a month, which is plenty for a VPN or a text-based site. It is not enough for serving video, running a download mirror, or hosting game assets.
